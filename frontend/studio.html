Our Story

NX Studio was founded in 2018 as a specialized creative division within IFX Group, born from a simple conviction: that premium brands deserve premium creative output — not templated, outsourced, or rushed work.

What began as a small team of three — a director, a designer, and a strategist — has evolved into a 24-person creative studio operating with cinema-grade equipment, enterprise-level software, and a portfolio spanning automotive, fashion, technology, finance, and beyond.

Our creative philosophy is simple: every brand has a unique story that deserves to be told with honesty, craft, and cinematic power. We build the visual infrastructure brands need to lead their markets.
